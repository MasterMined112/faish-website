Boats capsize and people drown. Going out on the water can seriously injure or kill you and the people with you, even on a day FaiSH grades as clear. No app removes that risk.

FaiSH is a planning aid. It is not the official forecast, and it does not decide whether your trip is safe.

The main points

This is not a forecast

FaiSH grades a forecast against your boat. It does not make one. It reads the Bureau of Meteorology's forecast grid near the coast, and Open-Meteo's models for wave period, past the grid and where the grid has no figure. The Bureau of Meteorology is the official forecaster for Australia. Check its marine forecast and warnings before you go. Where they differ from FaiSH, the Bureau of Meteorology is right and FaiSH is wrong.

Your limits set the grade

The boat form suggests limits from your boat's length and hull. That is a judgement, not a maker's rating, so check each figure. Once saved they are your limits, tightened for the kind of trip. Set them too high and FaiSH gives a confident answer that is wrong for your boat, with no way to know.

There is a lot it cannot see

The grade reads forecast wind, gusts and sea height on squares 5.5 to 13 km across, with a second weather model as a check. It leaves out thunderstorms, fog, coastal bars, currents, depth, rocks, debris, wind building across open water, sea height where none is forecast, your fuel and your crew, and every warning but the Bureau's marine wind warnings. The tide is the Bureau's prediction for the nearest station, blind to wind and air pressure, and not graded. Look at the water from the ramp.

The decision is yours

FaiSH informs. It will not call for help and will not decide whether you go. The words on the card describe the day against your limits. They are not advice that it is safe, and not an instruction. In an emergency call 000 or use VHF channel 16.

The Watch texts the people you name

If you start a Watch and have not ended it by your time back, FaiSH texts you, then the people you named. It does not contact marine rescue or the police, it sends no position, and a text can be late or never arrive, so log on with marine rescue as well. It keeps your mobile number, your contacts' names and numbers and each trip's details on FaiSH's server until you delete your Watch account. faish.app/privacy has the detail.

More on the grade

On open water it also reads how close together the waves are. A Bureau marine wind warning closes the hours it covers. Water with no Bureau marine area, such as a dam, has no warnings. With a place set, it counts the roughest water on the way. It stops at last light, and for a jet ski covers only the hours the state allows. How much a trip tightens your limits, and where caution starts, are FaiSH's judgement. A copy of the forecast on your phone can be old: the foot of Tomorrow says how old.

The other parts

An AI model writes the written reading, and it can be wrong, so go by the card. Bite windows and fish lists are a judgement. Bag and size limits are not in FaiSH, closures can be out of date, and the rules are your state's. Ramps and places come from government lists and can be closed, wrong or too shallow. Jet ski and motor rules cover some waters, not all. Maps are not for navigation. The line to a place is straight and can cross land. Marks stay on your iPhone with no backup. Any of it can be late, wrong or unavailable, so have another way to check.

What is up to you

The person in charge of a boat answers for it and everyone aboard, whatever any app says. If that is you, tell them the risk, and that FaiSH does not decide. Carry the gear the law asks for, log on with your state's marine rescue service, and keep to the fishing and boating rules. Do not read FaiSH while driving a boat or a car.
